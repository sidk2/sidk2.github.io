Bio
======
Hi, I'm Sid. I'm a fourth year Physics and Computing student at the University of California, Santa Barbara. My primary research interests are (in no particular order) a] **generative machine learning**, and its connections to physics and b] **statistical physics** (in particular lattice models). I am also interested in field theories (classical and quantum) and numerical methods for computational physics.
